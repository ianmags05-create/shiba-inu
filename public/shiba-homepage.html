Before you stay

Help us make their stay safe and happy

A little preparation keeps every fur guest comfortable. Here is what to pack and what to tell us.

Things to bring

Please prepare the following for your fur baby.

  • Vaccination recordsEvidence of vaccination for contagious diseases within the last year. First time only.
  • Rabies vaccinationEvidence of a rabies vaccination within the last year. First time only.
  • Valid IDA form of ID confirming your current address, such as a driver’s licence.
  • Their regular foodThe food they usually eat, so their stomach is not upset by a sudden change.
  • LeashThe leash you use for walks.
  • Comfort itemA favourite blanket with familiar smells, and any toy that helps them settle.

Things to be aware of

For the safety and well-being of all our fur guests.

  • On heat during their stayIf your pet is in season we will place them in their own separate room, away from other pets.
  • Cancelling a bookingPlease contact us as soon as possible if you need to cancel.
  • Do not forget the certificatesContagious disease and rabies vaccine certificates are needed at check in.
  • Food in meal portionsBring the food they always eat, already separated into single meals. Extra charges apply if we have to provide food.
  • Blanket and comfortTheir blanket, favourite toy and anything else that makes them feel safe.

Our story

It began with one Shiba Inu called Kumi

I always admired Shibas for being loyal, clever and stubbornly independent. When I finally found a breeder rehoming puppies, I fell for one of them on the spot and named her Kumi.

A friend’s success with breeding got me thinking, but I wanted to build something of my own. So I spent a long time researching how pet businesses run in Japan and Korea, and landed on an idea: breed one breed properly, and wrap a complete pet-care business around it.

That is what this is. Not just somewhere to buy a puppy, but puppies, supplies, professional grooming and a 24 hour hotel in one place, so owners never have to worry about who is looking after their pet.
